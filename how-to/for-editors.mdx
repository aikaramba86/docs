Everything an editor does in Zorlo. The board works on a phone and a desktop; this page shows the phone.

<Note>
Last checked: 30 October 2026, Zorlo release 3. Editors.
</Note>

## Sign in

The studio emails you an invitation. Open it, set a password or choose **Continue with Google**, and enter the six-digit code sent to your email. You do not need an authenticator app. If you edit for more than one studio, tap the studio name at the top to switch.

## Find your way around

You have two places: **Board** and **My details**. At the top of the board, **Board**, **Overview** and **Updates** switch pages, and the **bell** is your inbox.

![The board on a phone: your items at the top, then the columns.](/images/editor-board-phone.png)

- Your own items sit above the board: a task overdue or due soon, changes requested, or the client's song list arrived. Tap **Open card**. The list is empty when there is nothing to chase.
- **Me** shows your cards, **Everyone** the whole board. You start on Me.
- The chip row (To do 3, In progress 3 ...) jumps to a column. Columns scroll sideways.
- **Search** finds a task by its title, the couple's name or the booking number. **Filter** narrows the board. **Hidden** holds the cards waiting on the client and the delivered ones.

## Claim a task

1. Switch to **Everyone** and find a card with a dashed circle (nobody assigned).
2. Tap its **...** and choose **Assign to me**. It now shows under **Me**.

The studio can also assign work to you; you get an email and an inbox item each time. **Unassign me** gives a card back.

## Work a task

Tap a card to open it. The **Next step** panel at the top always shows what to do now.

![A task with changes requested: the note from the studio, the review link box, and the booking's reference material below.](/images/editor-task-phone.png)

1. **Start editing** moves the card to In progress.
2. Read **Notes for the editor** (what the studio wants) and **From the booking**: the song for this film and the full song list, group shots on photo tasks, the coverage brief with must-capture moments, important people and the run sheet, the events with their venues, the reference files, and the booking's other deliverables so you know what comes before what. Tap a card to expand it. Everything there is read only.
3. Use **Comments** to ask the studio anything. The booking's admin is named under Details.

You can also move a card from the board: **...**, then **Move to**. Hold a card to drag it. You can move only your own cards, and only between To do, In progress and Reviewing.

## Send for review

1. Paste the review link (Pixieset, Vimeo, a Drive folder) into the box. A link is required.
2. Tap **Send for review**. The card moves to Reviewing and the booking's admin gets an email with the link.

You cannot pull a card back from Reviewing. If you sent it too early, say so in a comment and the studio moves it back.

If the studio has switched review off, there is no Reviewing column: **Mark completed** finishes the card yourself.

## When changes are requested

You get an email and an inbox item with the studio's note, and the card comes back to In progress marked "Changes requested". The note shows in the Next step panel. Make the changes, paste the new link and **Send for review** again.

Once the studio approves, the card moves to Completed and you get an email. Nothing more to do on it.

## Your inbox and Updates

The **bell** shows a dot when something is addressed to you: assigned, changes requested, the client's input arrived, approved, due in 7 days, overdue. Tap an item to open the task. **Updates** lists what happened on your tasks and on unassigned ones. **Overview** shows your own counts.

## What you cannot do

- Move someone else's card, or move any card to Completed while review is on.
- Change a card's priority or due date, or add tasks. Ask the studio.
- See prices, payments, the client's phone or email, crew, meetings or notes. They are not in your view at all. See [Roles](/reference/roles).

## Update your details

**My details** holds your name and phone. Change them and tap **Save**. Your email is set by the invitation. **Theme** and **Sign out** are at the bottom.
