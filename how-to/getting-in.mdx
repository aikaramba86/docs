Getting an account, signing in and keeping it secure. Zorlo is invitation only: there is no sign-up page.

<Note>
Last checked: 30 October 2026, Zorlo release 2. Everyone.
</Note>

## Accept an invitation

You get an email "Name invited you to Studio". The link works for 7 days and only once.

**With a password**

1. Open the link. Set a password of at least 10 characters. Zorlo refuses passwords that have appeared in known leaks.
2. Enter the 6-digit code that Zorlo emails you. It expires after 10 minutes. You can ask for another after 60 seconds.
3. Owners and admins then set up two-step sign-in (below). Shooters go straight to their jobs.

**With Google**

1. Open the link and choose **Continue with Google**.
2. Use the Google account whose address matches the invitation. Any other Google account is refused, and the page says which address the invitation went to.
3. No code is needed. Owners and admins set up two-step sign-in next.

If the link has expired, ask the person who invited you to send a new one. If the invitation says it is for someone else, you opened it with the wrong account.

## Set up two-step sign-in

Owners and admins must. Shooters may skip it.

1. Install an authenticator app on your phone if you do not have one, for example Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password.
2. On the **Set up two-step sign-in** page, scan the QR code with the app, or type the key shown.
3. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows.

Keep the app: you need a fresh code every time you sign in. If you lose your phone, the studio owner can remove you and invite you again, or contact Zorlo.

## Sign in

![The sign-in page. Email first, then password or Google.](/images/login.png)

1. Go to the app and enter your email, then **Continue**.
2. Enter your password and click **Log in**, or choose **Continue with Google**.
3. Owners and admins enter the code from their authenticator app.

The sign-in page never says whether an email has an account. A wrong email and a wrong password look the same.

## Reset your password

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your email. If it has an account, a code arrives by email.
3. Enter the code and set a new password.

Resetting signs you out of every other device. If you usually use Google, sign in with Google instead: there is no password to reset.

## Add a password to a Google account, or Google to a password account

Both can be on one account. Sign in the way you already do, then use the other way next time with the same email: Zorlo links them. A password can be added with Forgot password.

## Install Zorlo on your phone

Zorlo is a web app that installs like an app.

- **iPhone:** open Zorlo in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open Zorlo in Chrome, tap the menu, then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

It then opens full screen from your home screen. Updates arrive on their own.

## Switch between light and dark

Zorlo starts in dark mode. Owners and admins open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose a theme. Shooters use **Theme** on My details. "System" follows your device.

## Sign out

Owners and admins: account menu, **Sign out**. Shooters: My details, **Sign out**. Signing out on one device leaves other devices signed in.

## Change your email address

Not possible from the app in this release. Ask the studio owner to invite your new address, then have the old membership removed.
