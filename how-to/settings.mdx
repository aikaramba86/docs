Settings is where the owner shapes the studio: packages, the lists every form uses, the defaults, who can do what, and the people. Open it from **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar. Admins see only the pages the owner has opened to them.

<Note>
Last checked: 30 October 2026, Zorlo release 2. Owner, and admins where noted.
</Note>

## Packages

Owner, or admins with Packages and add-ons.

![The packages list.](/images/settings-packages.png)

**Add a package**

1. Settings, **Packages**, then **New package**.
2. **Details**: name, an optional category and description, and whether it is **Available for new bookings**.
3. **Pricing**: choose **Fixed**, **By hours** or **Custom**. For Fixed, add one price or several named options (Weekday, Weekend). For By hours, add a row per number of hours with its price. Custom has no price.
4. **Included events**: add each event the package covers with its event type, hours and the crew needed by role and level. Leave the level as Any level when anyone with that role will do.
5. **Deliverables**: what the package hands over after the shoot, from your deliverable types, each with a quantity and whether it is **Complimentary**.
6. **Add-ons**: optional extras with a price.
7. Click **Save package**.

![A fixed-price package with two price options, two events and two add-ons.](/images/settings-package-fixed.png)

**Edit a package.** Click it in the list, change what you need and click Save package. Bookings already created from it do not change.

**Retire a package.** Switch off **Available for new bookings**, or use **Archive** from its row. It stays on old bookings.

See [Package pricing modes](/reference/package-pricing-modes) for how each mode behaves on a booking.

## Event types, lost reasons and vendor types

Owner, or admins with Lists. All three pages work the same way. A new studio starts with common entries in each list; change them as you like.

1. Open the page under Settings.
2. **Add** a new item by typing its name.
3. Use the **...** menu on an item to **Rename**, **Move up**, **Move down** or **Archive** it.
4. **Archived** shows retired items, each with **Restore**. An item that was never used can be deleted, with a few seconds to Undo; one in use can only be archived, so old bookings keep their labels.

| List | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| Event types | The kinds of event on bookings and packages, for example Nikah, Reception, Henna night, ROM, Pre-wedding |
| Lost reasons | The reason picked when an enquiry is marked lost |
| Vendor types | The type of each vendor on a booking: Make-up artist, Decor, Catering and so on |

## Deliverable types

Owner, or admins with Lists. Settings, **Deliverable types**: what the studio hands over after a shoot. The list is managed like the ones above, and each type has a few more details.

1. Click **Add deliverable type**.
2. **Name** it, for example Photo gallery, Album, Frames, Full summary film, Highlight film, Teaser.
3. **Waits on**: **Nothing**, **Song list** (films) or **Photo selection** (an album or frames the client picks photos for). An item that waits on something is not due until it arrives.
4. For a film, choose which **Film** it is: Full summary, Highlight or Teaser. The song list asks about the films a booking has.
5. **Usual turnaround** in days. Empty means the studio's Default delivery turnaround.
6. Click **Add type**.

![Deliverable types, with what each waits on and its turnaround.](/images/settings-deliverable-types.png)

A type on a package or a booking can be archived but not deleted. See [Deliverables](/how-to/deliverables).

## Crew roles and levels

Owner, or admins with Lists.

![Roles and levels. A shooter's own roles, level and rate are set on their profile.](/images/settings-crew.png)

**Roles** are the disciplines, for example Photographer and Videographer. **Levels** rank experience, for example Lead, Second and Third, in order. Both are managed like the lists above. Each shooter's roles, level per role and hourly rate are set on their crew profile, not here.

## Studio settings

Owner, or admins with Studio settings. Only the owner renames the studio or changes "Show estimated earnings to crew".

1. Settings, **Studio**.
2. Change the values and click **Save changes**.

![Studio settings with the defaults.](/images/settings-studio.png)

The page is in groups: the studio's name, logo and deposit; enquiries; crew call time, pay and travel time; crew reply reminders; **Client forms** (how long links work, the brief and song list reminders, and the intro and thank-you text each form shows), when client forms are on for the studio; and **Deliverables** (the photo selection reminder and the default turnaround). Every setting and its default is in [Studio settings](/reference/studio-settings) under Reference.

## Message templates

Owner, or admins with Studio settings, when Message templates is switched on for the studio. They live on the **Messages** page in the sidebar, not under Settings. See [Messages](/how-to/messages).

## Admin permissions

Owner only. Settings, **Admin permissions**. Each switch opens an area to every admin at once. All are off to begin with.

![The five switches.](/images/settings-admin-permissions.png)

What each one opens is in [Roles](/reference/roles). Why there are switches rather than per-person permissions is in [Admin permissions and booking sections](/explanation/admin-permissions-and-sections).

## Booking sections

Owner only. Settings, **Booking sections**. Switch off the parts of a booking your studio does not use: Song list, Group shots, Vendors, Coverage brief, Files, Client meetings, Deliverables.

![Booking sections. Off hides the section everywhere, including from crew. Nothing is deleted.](/images/settings-booking-sections.png)

A section the platform has not allowed for your studio does not appear here at all. With Deliverables off, new bookings get no deliverables from their package.

## Invite someone

Owners invite anyone. Admins invite shooters.

1. Go to **Crew** and click **Invite**.
2. Enter their **Name** and **Email**, and choose the **Role**: Crew, Admin or Owner.
3. For crew, add their crew roles with level and hourly rate now, or later on their profile.
4. Send. They get an email with a link that works for 7 days.

![The Crew list with roles, crew roles, confirmed upcoming jobs and status.](/images/crew-list.png)

Zorlo refuses to invite, as owner or admin, someone who already belongs to another studio, and refuses to invite another studio's owner or admin in any role. Someone already invited cannot be invited again as a different role. The message says why. Until they accept, their row shows Invited: use its menu to **Cancel invitation** or send a fresh link.

## Edit a crew profile

Owner, or admins with Crew pay for the roles, levels and rates.

1. Go to **Crew** and open the person.
2. **Studio role** (owner only): Crew, Admin or Owner.
3. **Crew roles**: add a role with **Add a crew role**, set its **Level** and **Hourly rate**. A person can have several roles with a different level for each. Click **Save**.
4. The left side shows their upcoming jobs and the dates they have marked they cannot work. Only the person changes those dates, under My details.

![A shooter's profile.](/images/crew-profile.png)

The hourly rate is copied onto each place they are given, where it can be changed for that job alone.

## Remove someone from the studio

Owner only. Open the person and click **Remove from studio**. Their places on future events are released and they lose access at once. Their name stays on past bookings and their deliverables stay assigned until someone changes them.
