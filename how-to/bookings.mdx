A booking is one client engagement: the client, the package and price, the events, the crew, the payments and everything the crew need on the day. This page covers creating and changing bookings. What you hand over is on [Deliverables](/how-to/deliverables), and the forms the client fills in on [Client forms](/how-to/client-forms). Crew are on [Crew on an event](/how-to/crew), money on [Payments](/how-to/payments).

<Note>
Last checked: 30 October 2026, Zorlo release 2. Owners and admins.
</Note>

![The Bookings list. Upcoming, Past and All, with filters for status, admin and event date.](/images/bookings-list.png)

## Create a booking from a package

1. Click **New booking**.
2. Enter the client (see [Enquiries](/how-to/enquiries) for the client fields).
3. Under **Package**, choose one. Pick its price option (for example Weekday or Weekend, or a number of hours) and tick any add-ons.
4. **Agreed price** is filled in from the option plus add-ons. Change it if you agreed something else. **Deposit** is suggested from the studio's percentage.
5. Under **Booking**, set the status (Enquiry, Quoted or Confirmed), the assigned admin and a follow-up date.
6. Under **Events**, the package's events are already listed with their crew needed. Fill in each event's date, times and venue. Tick **Main event** on the one the balance is due on.
7. Add a first note and click **Create booking**.

![The new booking form.](/images/booking-new.png)

A booking created as Confirmed needs at least one event. The package is copied onto the booking, deliverables included: changing the package later in Settings does not change this booking.

## Set a custom price

Choose a package with Custom pricing, or any package, and type the **Agreed price** yourself. Published prices are starting points.

## Open a booking

Click it in **Bookings** or search by name, phone or email. The page has the events and sections on the left and the **Booking details** panel on the right: status, assigned admin, source, follow-up date, package, agreed price, deposit and balance due date. Click a value in the panel to change it. Changes save as you go. **Copy message** at the top fills one of the studio's message templates with this booking's details (see [Messages](/how-to/messages)).

![A confirmed booking with two events.](/images/booking-detail.png)

## Change the package

1. In the right-hand panel, click **Change** next to the package.
2. Choose the new package, its price option and add-ons. The agreed price is suggested; change it if needed.
3. Under **Events to add**, tick the package events you want added. Events already on the booking are marked. Deliverables to add or remove are listed the same way.
4. Click **Change package**.

![Changing the package shows what would change before anything is saved.](/images/booking-change-package.png)

Nothing changes silently. Crew places with someone on them are kept.

## Add or edit an event

1. Click **+** next to **Events**, or the pencil on an event card.
2. Fill in the **Event type**, **Date**, **Starts** and **Ends**.
3. Under **Venue**, start typing to pick a saved venue, or type a new name. Enter the postal code and click **Look up** to fill the address.
4. Under **Crew needed**, add a row per role and level with how many you need.
5. **Notes for crew** is shown to the crew on their job page. **Internal notes** never are.
6. Save.

![Adding an event.](/images/booking-add-event.png)

**If you change the date or times** of an event that has confirmed crew, Zorlo first shows who would no longer be free, then asks every confirmed person to reconfirm. See Crew on an event.

## Getting-ready stops

For weddings where crew start at the bride's or groom's home before the venue:

1. Edit the event and switch on **Getting ready** at the bottom.
2. Add the bride's side and the groom's side, each with a place, address and start time.
3. On the crew panel, each person can be set to start at a stop or at the venue.

Needs attention flags a stop with crew on it but no address.

## Cancel one event

Click the pencil on the event, then **Cancel this event**, and give a reason. Its crew places are released and everyone on them is emailed. The rest of the booking carries on.

## Add contacts

Who the crew should call on the day: the planner, a family member.

In **Contacts**, click **Add contacts**. Each contact has a role label (for example Bride side, Planner), a name and a phone. Crew see these on their job page.

## Add the coverage brief, group shots, vendors and song list

These sections appear only when the studio has them switched on (Settings, Booking sections).

- **Coverage brief**: click **Add the brief** and enter the must-capture moments, important people with their relation, reference links, special notes and the run sheet (time and activity). Crew see all of it.
- **Group shots**: click **Add group shots** and list the family and friend combinations to photograph, in order, each optionally tied to one event. Crew see them.
- **Vendors**: click **Add vendors** and enter each vendor's type (from the studio's list), name, Instagram handle and phone. Crew see them.
- **Song list**: click **Add songs** and enter the songs for each film the booking's deliverables include (the full summary film ranked, one each for the highlight and the teaser), plus the couple's names as they want them written. Crew never see the song list. Saving it starts the films that were waiting on it.
- **Deliverables** and **Client forms** have their own pages: [Deliverables](/how-to/deliverables) and [Client forms](/how-to/client-forms).

## Add files and Drive folders

In **Files**, pick the kind (run sheet, seating plan, reference, family combinations, other), click **Add files** and choose up to 10 images or PDFs of 10 MB each. Crew on the booking can open them. **Add Drive folder** links a Google Drive folder by its address. The **Song list document** box below takes the couple's filled-in Word or PDF song list, which crew never see. Files a client adds through the coverage brief form land here too, marked as from the client.

## Record a client meeting

In **Client meetings**, click **Add meeting**: face to face or virtual, start and end, location or meeting link, notes, and which owners or admins are attending. The meeting shows on the schedule as a Meeting block for the whole team. Crew never see meetings. A clash for a tagged person is a warning, never a block.

## Change the status by hand

Click **Status** in the right-hand panel. From Enquiry or Quoted you can go to Enquiry, Quoted or Confirmed. Confirming needs a logged deposit unless you are the owner. From Confirmed onwards you can move through Shot, Editing, Delivered and Closed. See [Booking statuses](/reference/booking-statuses).

## Reassign the booking

Click **Assigned admin** in the right-hand panel and choose another admin. Needs attention items and decline emails for the booking go to the new person.

## Cancel a booking

Owner or the booking's assigned admin, on a confirmed booking.

1. Click **Cancel booking** at the bottom of the right-hand panel.
2. Give a **Reason**.
3. Choose what happens to the deposit: **Kept**, **Refunded** (Zorlo logs a refund payment) or **Moved to a new date** (say which date in the reason).
4. Click **Yes, cancel booking**.

![Cancelling needs a reason and a deposit outcome.](/images/booking-cancel-dialog.png)

Every event still to come is cancelled, crew places are released and everyone on them is emailed. Payments already logged stay on record. Client form links stop working and the deliverables are locked. The booking shows as Cancelled.

![A cancelled booking keeps its history.](/images/booking-cancelled.png)

## Reopen a cancelled booking

Open the booking and click **Reopen booking** in the red box at the top. It goes back to the status it had and its events are scheduled again. Crew places stay open and nobody is emailed: offer them again yourself.

## See a booking's history

Expand **Activity** at the bottom of the booking. Every change is listed with who made it and when.
