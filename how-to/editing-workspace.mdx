Where the editing happens after the shoot. Every open deliverable of a booking at Editing becomes a card on a board. Editors move their cards along, you review and approve, and a finished card tells you the item is ready to deliver.

<Note>
Last checked: 30 October 2026, Zorlo release 3. Owners and admins. Editing needs the [Deliverables](/how-to/deliverables) section. Nothing on this page shows until it is on.
</Note>

## Switch to the Editing workspace

You have two workspaces: **Studio** (bookings, schedule and crew) and **Editing** (the board). Staff with a crew role have a third, **Jobs**.

1. Click the studio name at the top of the sidebar. On a phone, open **More** and tap the studio name at the top of the sheet.
2. Choose **Editing**. The menu changes to **Board** and **My details**, and the board opens.

Choose **Studio** the same way to go back. Links do it for you too: **View on board** on a booking takes you into Editing, and **Open booking** on a card takes you back.

![The workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar.](/images/editing-switcher.png)

## Invite an editor

1. Studio workspace, **Crew**, **Invite**.
2. Enter their email and choose the role **Editor**. Admins can invite editors as well as crew.
3. Send. They get the usual invitation email, set a password or continue with Google, and enter the sign-in code. Editors do not need an authenticator app.

To turn an existing member into an editor, open their profile and change **Studio role** to Editor (owner only). Zorlo refuses with "Remove them from their upcoming jobs first." while they hold a place on an upcoming event; their crew roles and rates are cleared once saved. Editors appear in their own **Editors** group on the Crew page.

**Editing skills.** On an editor's profile (and on owners and admins), tick **Photo** or **Video** under Editing skills. The assignee menus then list people who match the task first, under "Matches this task".

## Read the board

![The board for everyone: cards in To do, In progress, Reviewing and Completed.](/images/editing-board.png)

- Four columns: **To do**, **In progress**, **Reviewing** and **Completed**. **Hidden columns** holds two more that only Zorlo moves cards into: **Waiting on client** (a film waiting on the song list, an album waiting on the photo selection) and **Delivered**.
- Each card is one task: its title, the couple and booking number, a problem line when there is one ("Changes requested", "Waiting on song list"), the due date (red when overdue), a link icon when a review link is set, the comment count and the assignee's initial. A dashed circle means nobody is assigned. **Urgent** shows on the right; Normal and Low show nothing.
- **Me / Everyone** shows your own cards or the whole board. Staff start on Everyone.
- **Search** matches the title, the couple's name and the booking number. **Filter** narrows by assignee, priority, type (photo, film, physical), booking, overdue only and changes requested only. Active filters show as pills with **Clear all**.
- **Display** chooses what each card shows (booking line, priority, assignee, due date, type, created date). Remembered on this device.
- On a phone, the columns sit side by side and scroll sideways. A row of chips with counts jumps to a column. Hold a card to drag it; hold it at the screen edge to move to the next column.

## Where cards come from

- When you move a booking to **Editing**, Zorlo makes one card per deliverable that is not yet delivered. The card's column follows the deliverable's status: Not started becomes To do, In progress stays In progress, Ready becomes Completed, Waiting on client goes to the hidden Waiting column.
- A deliverable added to a booking already at Editing gets its card at once. Removing the deliverable removes the card; Undo brings both back.
- The card and the deliverable are one thing. Move the card and the deliverable's status follows; change the deliverable on the booking and the card moves.
- When the song list or photo selection arrives, the waiting card moves to To do and its assignee is told.
- Marking a deliverable **Delivered** or **Collected** on the booking moves its card to the hidden Delivered column.
- Moving a booking back to Shot, or cancelling it, parks its cards. They return, as they were, when the booking is at Editing again or reopened.

## Assign, prioritise and move

Open the card's **...** menu, or drag the card.

- **Move to** lists the columns the card may go to. Dragging does the same.
- **Assignee** lists editors first ("Matches this task", then "Everyone else"), each with how many open tasks they hold. Owners and admins can hold cards too. The new assignee gets an email.
- **Priority**: Urgent, Normal or Low. Editors see it but cannot change it.
- The due date is the deliverable's. Change it on the booking with **Edit** on the deliverable. An extra task has its own date (below).
- Reorder cards within a column by dragging. The order is shared by everyone.
- The column's **...** menu has **Sort by priority** and **Sort by due date**. Sorting changes the order for everyone.

## Review and approve

Click a card to open it. On a desktop it opens as a dialog; on a phone as its own page.

![A task waiting for review: the stage track, the review link, Approve and Request changes, and the booking's reference material on the right.](/images/editing-task-dialog.png)

The **Next step** panel always shows the one thing to do now. When an editor sends a card for review, you get an email and the panel shows the review link.

1. Open the review link and check the edit.
2. **Approve** moves the card to Completed and sets the deliverable to Ready. The notice "Approved. Moved to Completed" has **Undo** for five seconds, since a card cannot be pulled back from Completed by hand.
3. Or **Request changes**, with a note saying what to change. The card returns to In progress marked "Changes requested", and the editor gets an email and an inbox item with your note.

Also on the task:

- **Notes for the editor**: what you want them to know ("warmer grade", "skip the groom's uncle"). Editors read it.
- **Comments**: a thread between you and the editor. Your change requests and approvals land here too.
- **Activity**: who did what and when, folded by default.
- **Details**: status, assignee, priority, due date, the booking's admin, type and dates.
- **From the booking**: the reference material the editor sees, read only: the song for this film (and the whole song list), group shots on photo tasks, the coverage brief, the events, files of kind reference, family combinations and run sheet, and the booking's other deliverables. Never prices, payments or contact details.

If the owner switches **Review required** off (Settings, Studio, Editing), the Reviewing column disappears and editors complete their own cards. You get an email when they do.

## Deliver

A Completed card says **Ready to deliver**. Mark the item delivered on the booking as usual (Deliverables, the row's **...**, **Delivered**, with the delivery link). The card leaves the board. The booking's Deliverables card shows **In review** while a card is in Reviewing, **View on board** on each row, and "All edits completed" once every card is done. The booking's own status stays where you left it.

Needs attention shows "Highlight film ready to deliver" to the booking's admin as a reminder.

## Add a task that is not a deliverable

For work like "Export 20 sneak peeks for Instagram" or "Rename and back up raw files".

1. Click **New task** at the right of the toolbar, or **+** on the To do column, or press n.
2. Pick the **Booking** by the couple's name or number. Only bookings at Editing, Delivered or Closed are offered.
3. Give it a **Title**, and if you like a description, an assignee, a priority and a due date.
4. **Add task**. It goes to the top of To do and the assignee is emailed.

![New task: a booking, a title and the optional details.](/images/editing-new-task.png)

An extra task has no type and changes nothing on the booking when it is completed. Change its title, description or due date with **Edit task** on the card; **Remove** hides it at once with Undo for five seconds.

## Overview, Updates and the inbox

The toolbar's **Board**, **Overview** and **Updates** links switch pages.

![Overview: open, in review, overdue, due soon and completed, then by editor, by type and the bookings in editing.](/images/editing-overview.png)

- **Overview** counts open, in review, overdue, due in 14 days and completed in the last 30 days, for Me or Everyone. Below: each editor's open and overdue count, the same by type, and every booking in editing with its open tasks and earliest due date. Click a tile or a booking to open the board filtered to it.
- **Updates** is the studio's editing activity, newest first, grouped by day: added, assigned, moved, changes requested, approved, released.
- The **bell** at the top right (in the Editing workspace) is your inbox: tasks assigned to you, changes requested on them, their client input arriving, approvals, and your own tasks due in 7 days or overdue. A dot shows until you open it. Click an item to open the task.

![Updates: everything that happened on the board, by day.](/images/editing-updates.png)

## Keyboard shortcuts

On a desktop, while nothing is typing: **/** searches, **n** opens New task, **?** lists the shortcuts, and **Enter** opens the focused card.

## Editing settings

Settings, **Studio**, under **Editing**. Owner, or admins with Studio settings. See [Studio settings](/reference/studio-settings).

![The Editing group in Studio settings.](/images/settings-editing.png)

## What Zorlo watches for you

Needs attention tells the booking's assigned admin about work **waiting for review** (after the review reminder, or Urgent when the item is due within 3 days), **not started** (To do with nobody assigned, or still in To do after the not-started reminder), **unassigned** two days after the card was made, and **ready to deliver**. The deliverable items (overdue, due soon, song list and photo selection not received) stay as they were. See [Needs attention rules](/reference/needs-attention-rules).
