Putting people on the crew places of an event, and dealing with replies. Each task assumes you have the booking open (Bookings, then the client's name).

<Note>
Last checked: 30 October 2026, Zorlo release 2. Owners and admins.
</Note>

## Open the crew panel for an event

1. On the booking, find the event card and click **Crew**.
2. A panel opens on the right with every place on that event, its role and level, who is on it and their status.

![The crew panel for an event. One place is still Open, one is Offered.](/images/booking-crew-sheet.png)

If an event has no crew places yet, click the pencil on the event card, add rows under **Crew needed**, and save. The Crew button then appears.

## Offer a place to a shooter

1. Open the crew panel and click **Assign** on an open place.
2. Zorlo lists everyone with that role. People who are free come first, ordered by how well their level matches. People who are busy at that time are greyed out with "Busy".
3. Type part of a name to narrow the list. Someone who has been invited but has not accepted yet is named, but cannot be chosen until they join.
4. Click the person, then **Offer**.

![Choosing a second videographer. Both are free. The level and hourly rate come from their profile.](/images/booking-crew-picker.png)

**What happens next.** The place shows Offered. The person gets an email and sees the job under Requests on their phone, with Confirm and Decline. Their calendar is not blocked yet. If they have not replied after the studio's reminder window (default 48 hours), Zorlo emails them once more. If there is still no reply after a further window (default 24 hours), the booking appears in Needs attention.

## Confirm a place on a shooter's behalf

Use this when a shooter has agreed by phone or WhatsApp and you do not want to wait for them to tap Confirm.

1. Open the crew panel and click **Assign** on the open place.
2. Click the person, then **Confirm on their behalf**.

The place goes straight to Confirmed and the person's calendar is blocked for the event. They get no offer email. They see the job under Upcoming.

## Remove an offer

1. Open the crew panel.
2. On the Offered row, click **Remove**.

The place goes back to Open. The person is not emailed, because they had not confirmed anything.

## Send a reminder by hand

1. Go to Needs attention.
2. On the row "Name hasn't replied to the offer", click **Send reminder**.

Zorlo sends the reminder email at once. You can do this at most once an hour per place.

## When someone declines

Nothing for you to click. The place reopens, the booking's assigned admin gets an email with the person's reason if they gave one, and the event shows "1 crew place to fill" in Needs attention. Offer the place to someone else as above.

## Take a confirmed shooter off an event

1. Open the crew panel.
2. On the Confirmed row, click **Withdraw**.
3. Give a reason and confirm.

The place reopens and the person's calendar is freed. They get an email saying they are off the event, with your note. If the event is within 48 hours, the withdrawal is recorded as late on the studio's own record.

A shooter can also withdraw themselves from their job page. You then get an email and an Urgent item in Needs attention.

## After a date or time change

When you change an event's date, start or end time, every confirmed shooter is asked to confirm again:

1. Save the event. Zorlo first shows which crew would no longer be free at the new time, so you can decide before saving.
2. Each confirmed place moves to **Reconfirm** and the person is emailed the old and new details. On their phone the request shows what changed, with the old date, times or venue struck through.
3. Their calendar is freed until they confirm again.
4. Needs attention shows "Name to reconfirm the new time" with a **Send reminder** button.

Changing only the venue or the notes for crew does not need reconfirmation. Confirmed crew get an update email and stay confirmed.

## Check that an event has a lead

If an event needs a role and nobody on it has the Lead level, the crew panel warns you. Zorlo does not block it: you decide.
