Message templates for each stage of a booking, filled in with the booking's details and copied into WhatsApp. Zorlo sends nothing to clients itself: you copy, check and paste.

<Note>
Last checked: 30 October 2026, Zorlo release 2. Owners and admins copy messages. The owner, or admins with Studio settings, edit templates. Message templates are switched on per studio by Zorlo. If there is no Messages item in the sidebar, ask for it.
</Note>

## The Messages page

**Messages** in the sidebar lists your templates grouped by stage, in the order a booking goes through them: Enquiry reply, Booking details request, Brief request, Song list request, Payment reminder, Delivery. A new studio starts with one plain template per stage. Click a template to read it on the right (on a phone it opens from the bottom).

![The Messages page.](/images/messages-page.png)

## Copy a message from a booking

1. Open the booking and click **Copy message** at the top.
2. Templates are on the left, with the stages that suit the booking's status first. Click one.
3. The filled message is on the right. Anything Zorlo could not fill is listed under **Missing** and highlighted in the text. Edit the text if you like: edits here never change the template.
4. Click **Copy** (or **Copy anyway** when something is missing) and paste it into WhatsApp.

![Copy message from a booking: templates on the left, the filled text on the right.](/images/booking-copy-message.png)

Copy message is also on **Needs attention**, as a small icon on items that have a matching template, for example Deposit not received with the Payment reminder. And on the Messages page, **Fill for a booking** lets you search a client's name and copy any template for them.

## Edit a template

1. On the Messages page, click a template and then **Edit template**, or click **New template**.
2. Give it a **Name**, choose its **Stage** and write the **Message**. Click **Insert** to drop in a placeholder where you want a detail to appear.
3. Save. A stage can have several templates, for example a formal and a casual enquiry reply.

![Editing a template, with the placeholders to insert.](/images/template-editor.png)

## Placeholders

| Placeholder | Fills with |
| --- | --- |
| Client name, Partner name, Couple names | From the client: the first name on the booking, their partner, or both joined with & |
| Studio name, Admin name | Your studio, and the booking's assigned admin |
| Event type, Event date, Venue | The booking's main event |
| Package, Agreed price, Deposit amount, Balance outstanding, Balance due date | The booking's package and money, written as the app shows money, for example \$4,800 |
| Booking details link, Coverage brief link, Song list link | The booking's live client form link. Make the link in Client forms first, or the message says so under Missing |
| Delivery links | Every delivery link on the booking's deliverables, one per line |

A placeholder your studio cannot use (a form link when client forms are off, delivery links when deliverables are off) stays as it was typed.
