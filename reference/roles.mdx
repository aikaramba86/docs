What an owner, an admin, a shooter and an editor can see and do. A person has one account and one role per studio.

<Note>
Last checked: 30 October 2026, Zorlo release 2.
</Note>

## The four roles

| Role | Can see | Can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything in the studio, including money totals | Everything: bookings, crew, payments, settings, people and their roles |
| Admin | Bookings, events, clients, schedule, crew, enquiries. Each booking's price and payments | Create and edit bookings, assign crew, log payments (add-ons and refunds too), attach proof and invoices, track deliverables, send client form links, copy messages, add notes. Invite shooters. More when the owner opens an area (below) |
| Shooter | Only the events they are offered or hold a place on, the crew notes and brief for those, the other crew's names and roles, and their own pay estimate | Confirm or decline an offer, withdraw from a job, set the dates they are not available, edit their own details |
| Editor | The editing board, and for each task the brief, the song list, group shots, events, reference files and the booking's other deliverables. Their own details | Claim unassigned tasks, move their own between To do, In progress and Reviewing, add a review link and comments. Only while Editing is switched on for the studio |

Shooters never see prices, payments, invoices, other crew's rates, internal notes, client meetings, deliverables, client form links or the song list. Editors never see prices, payments, client contact details, crew, meetings or notes. See [What editors can and cannot see](/explanation/what-editors-see).

## One studio, or several

- An owner or admin belongs to exactly one studio.
- A shooter or an editor can work for several studios. Each studio sees only its own jobs or tasks for that person, and sees a shooter's other commitments only as "busy", with no detail.
- Inviting someone who is already an owner or admin elsewhere is refused, and so is inviting an owner or admin to be a shooter or editor somewhere else.
- A member cannot be made an editor while they hold a place on an upcoming event. Admins can invite editors as they invite crew; changing an existing member's role is owner only. See [Why owners and admins belong to one studio](/explanation/one-studio).

## What the owner can open to admins

Settings, Admin permissions. Each switch applies to every admin of the studio at once. All are off for a new studio.

| Switch | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| Packages and add-ons | Create, edit and archive packages, their prices, events, crew and add-ons |
| Lists | Event types, lost reasons, vendor types, and crew roles and levels |
| Studio settings | The deposit, call time, reminders and other studio defaults. Not the studio name |
| Crew pay | Crew members' roles, levels and hourly rates on their profiles. Not anyone's studio role |
| Money totals | Totals across bookings and the Money page. Admins can always log payments |

Always owner only: the switches themselves, studio roles, removing people, renaming the studio, correcting or deleting a payment, confirming a booking without a deposit, deleting a booking or client, and the audit log.

## Who gets what on a booking

- Every booking has one assigned admin, responsible for it end to end. The owner can reassign it.
- Needs attention items for a booking go to its assigned admin. Admins see their own bookings' items first, with a switch for all. The owner sees everything.
- A decline or withdrawal emails the assigned admin.

## Staff who also shoot

An owner or admin with a crew role can be given a place on an event. They switch between the Studio and Jobs workspaces from the studio name at the top of the sidebar (or the top of More on a phone). They are not emailed about their own places, since they can see them in the studio. With Editing on, the same switcher opens the Editing workspace, where owners and admins can hold editing tasks themselves. See [Editing workspace](/how-to/editing-workspace).

## The platform admin

A separate role for the people who run Zorlo itself, not a studio role. See [Platform console](/reference/platform-console).
