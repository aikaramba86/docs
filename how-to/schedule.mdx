The schedule shows every shoot and client meeting, with the next two weeks listed beside it. The date check answers "are we free on this date?" before you reply to an enquiry.

<Note>
Last checked: 30 October 2026, Zorlo release 2. Owners and admins.
</Note>

## Read the calendar

On a desktop the schedule opens on the **Week** view, scrolled to the week's first shoot, with an **Upcoming** panel on the right. On a phone the calendar comes first and Upcoming sits below it.

![The week view with the Upcoming panel. The dashed block still has open places.](/images/schedule-week.png)

![On a phone the schedule opens on the list.](/images/schedule-phone.png)

Each block shows the event type, the client and the crew count, for example 2/4 means two of four places are confirmed.

| Looks like | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Solid block | A shoot with every place confirmed |
| Dashed border | A place is still open, offered or waiting for reconfirmation |
| Outline | An enquiry or quote, not confirmed yet |
| Speech-bubble block | A client meeting. Highlighted when you are tagged on it |
| Grey all-day block | A day a crew member has marked unavailable. Click it to see who |

Click **Key** for the same legend on screen. Click a shoot to open its crew panel. Click a meeting to open its card, which links to the booking.

## The Upcoming panel

Lists the next 14 days of shoots and meetings by day, each with the crew call time, venue, booking status and crew count. Two counts sit at the top: **Booked shoots** and **Open places on booked shoots**. Enquiries and quotes are shown apart as "+N not booked yet". The panel follows the calendar's filters, and clicking a row opens the same crew panel or meeting card.

## Switch views

Use the switch at the top: **Day**, **Week**, **Month** or **List**. Times are 24-hour. On a phone, List starts today and runs 30 days.

## Move around

**Today** jumps back to now. The arrows move a day, week or month at a time, or 30 days in the phone list.

## Filter by person or role

- **Crew**: show only shoots a particular person is on, in any status.
- **Role**: show only shoots that need a particular role, for example Videographer.
- **Enquiries**: show or hide enquiries and quotes.
- **Unavailable**: hide or show the days crew have marked unavailable. They are shown to begin with.
- **Meetings**: show or hide client meetings.

## Run a date check

1. Click **Check a date** at the top of the schedule or Needs attention, or go to **Date check** in the sidebar.
2. Enter the **Date**. Add **From** and **To** times if you know them, so travel time between events is taken into account.
3. Optionally choose a **Package**. Zorlo then checks whether that package's crew requirement can be met.
4. Click **Check**.

![The date check form.](/images/date-check.png)

## Read the result

![A date check result: free crew per role, who is busy, and what is already on that day.](/images/date-check-result.png)

The result has one card per crew role with a count of who is free and their names and levels. Expand **busy** under a card to see who is not free. Below that, **Already on that day** lists the events booked for that date with their times and crew count, and **Other enquiries for that day** shows open enquiries asking about the same date. With a package chosen, the result says plainly whether the crew needed can be met.

Zorlo never holds a date and never blocks a second enquiry. It shows the facts. You decide.

A free person means nobody has confirmed them for another event at that time (in any studio they work with, with the studio's travel buffer applied) and they have not marked the day unavailable. An offer that has not been confirmed does not make them busy.

## Check availability from a booking

The new booking form has a **Check availability** button on each event. It runs the same check for that event's date and times.
