<Note>
For owners and admins. About 20 minutes. Your studio needs at least one package, one event type and one shooter who has accepted their invitation.
</Note>

You will take one enquiry all the way: log it, check the date, quote, offer a place to a shooter, log the deposit and see the booking confirm. Use a made-up couple so you can delete nothing and keep it as a test.

## 1. Log the enquiry

1. From **Needs attention**, click **New booking**.
2. **Client**: Couple. Name "Test Couple", partner's name "Test Partner", phone 9000 0000. Under How they found you, type "Walkthrough".
3. **Package**: choose your package. Pick the Weekend price. Notice the **Agreed price** fills in and the **Deposit** is suggested.
4. **Booking**: leave the status as **Enquiry**. Set **Follow up on** to tomorrow.
5. **Events**: the package's events are listed with their crew needed. On the main event, set the date to a Saturday about three weeks away, 11:00 to 16:00, and type a venue name. Remove the other event with the cross if you want to keep this short.
6. **First note**: "Asked for the weekend price. Sent the brochure."
7. Click **Create booking**.

![The new booking form.](/images/booking-new.png)

You are on the booking's page. Its status chip says Enquiry. In the sidebar, Enquiries now counts one more. Under **Deliverables**, the package's gallery and films are already listed, waiting for the shoot.

## 2. Check the date

1. Click **Date check** in the sidebar.
2. Enter the event's date and times, choose your package and click **Check**.

The result lists, per role, who is free and who is busy, the events already on that day, and whether the package's crew can be met. Nothing is held. Go back to the booking.

## 3. Quote

1. In the right-hand panel, change **Status** to **Quoted**.
2. The agreed price and deposit are already there. Change them if you quoted something else.

Three days from now, if no deposit has been logged, Needs attention would show "Deposit not received" as Soon. You will not wait that long.

## 4. Offer a place to a shooter

1. On the event card, click **Crew**.
2. On the Photographer, Lead place, click **Assign**. Free shooters are listed first with their level.
3. Click your shooter and then **Offer**.

![Choosing a shooter for a place.](/images/booking-crew-picker.png)

The place shows Offered. The shooter gets an email and sees the job under Requests on their phone. Their calendar is not blocked yet.

If you want to see the other side, sign in as the shooter on a phone and tap **Confirm**. Or, back on the crew panel, click **Assign** on another open place, pick someone and choose **Confirm on their behalf** to see a place go straight to Confirmed.

## 5. Log the deposit

1. Scroll to **Payments** and click **Log payment**.
2. Type Deposit, the amount already filled in, today's date, method PayNow.
3. Click **Save payment**.

![Logging a payment.](/images/booking-log-payment.png)

Look at the status chip: **Confirmed**. Logging the deposit did that. The booking has left the Enquiries list and sits under Bookings, Upcoming. On the schedule it has changed from an outline to a solid block with a dashed border, because a place is still open.

## 6. Watch Needs attention

Go back to **Needs attention**. Because the event is more than 7 days away and inside the flag window, you see "N crew places to fill" as Soon, with an **Assign** button. Fill the places and the item disappears on its own. If a shooter has not replied, "Name hasn't replied to the offer" appears once the event is within 7 days, with **Send reminder**.

## 7. Tidy up

You can leave the test booking as it is, or open it and click **Cancel booking** with the reason "Walkthrough" and the deposit **Kept**. The shooter you offered gets a cancellation email, so tell them first, or remove the offer from the crew panel before cancelling.

## Done

You have seen the whole path: enquiry, date check, quote, crew offer, deposit, confirmed. Everything else in the [How-to guides](/how-to/getting-in) is a variation on these steps.
