Three forms the client fills in on their phone from a link you send them: **Booking details**, **Coverage brief** and **Song list**. Their answers land on the booking, and you decide what to do where they differ from yours.

<Note>
Last checked: 30 October 2026, Zorlo release 2. Owners and admins. Client forms are switched on per studio by Zorlo. If bookings have no Client forms section, ask for it to be switched on.
</Note>

## How a form link works

- One link per form per booking. A link works for 30 days (Settings, Studio, **Form links work for**) and stops working when you make a new one, switch it off, or the booking is lost or cancelled.
- The form opens already filled with what the booking holds for that form: names, contacts, event dates, times and venues, the brief, the songs. Never money, crew, fees, notes, meetings or other bookings.
- The client sends the form once. After that the link shows their answers read-only.
- Their answers fill in anything empty on the booking at once. Where the booking already had a value and the client gave a different one, nothing changes until you choose (below).
- Clients never choose a package or add events. Agree the package first, add every event, then send the link. If an event is added later, send a new link and it opens with the new event in it.

## Send a form

1. Open the booking and find **Client forms**. There is one row per form.
2. Click **Create link** on the row. For the song list, tick which films the client should choose songs for first (the booking's film deliverables are ticked for you).
3. Click **Copy link** and paste it into WhatsApp. If your studio has message templates, **Copy message** at the top of the booking fills the request template with the link for you.

The row now says **Waiting for client** with the date the link stops working.

![The Client forms section: one row per form. The brief has arrived with one change to check.](/images/booking-client-forms.png)

| Row says | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Not sent | No link has been made |
| Waiting for client | A link is out and the client has not sent the form |
| Received | The client sent it. Use View answers |
| Link expired | The link ran out before the client sent it. Create a new one |
| Switched off | You switched the link off, or the booking is lost or cancelled |

## When the client sends it

The booking's assigned admin gets an email, "Name sent their coverage brief", with how many changes there are to check and a button to the booking. Nothing from the form is in the email. If there is no assigned admin, the owners get it.

On the booking the row shows **Received**. Empty fields are already filled. If anything differs from what the booking had, Needs attention shows **Client changes to check**.

## Check the client's changes

1. On the form's row, click **View answers**.
2. The panel **What the client sent** lists every field with the booking's **Current** value and the **Client's** value. Fields that were filled in straight away say Used client's.
3. For each difference, click **Use client's** to take their value, or **Keep current** to leave the booking as it is. The client's value stays visible in the history either way.

A change to an event's date, times or venue is saved the same way as an edit on the booking: with crew on the event you choose whether to email them (a date or time change asks them to reconfirm, a venue change just tells them), and **Before you use it** shows which crew would no longer be free. Lists (contact persons, vendors, group shots, songs) are taken as a whole: the client's list replaces yours.

![View answers: the change to check at the top, then everything the client sent.](/images/booking-form-answers.png)

## Send a new link or switch one off

On the row's menu: **New link** makes a fresh link and the old one stops working (the client's earlier answers stay under **View earlier answers**). **Switch off link** stops the link without making a new one. A client who opens a dead link sees "This link no longer works".

## Files the client adds

On the brief, the client can add images and PDFs (an itinerary, seating plan, inspiration, family combinations). Each file lands in the booking's **Files** as it is added, marked as from the client, and counts towards the booking's 10 files. Clients cannot remove a file once added. You can, on the booking.

## Change the wording the client sees

Settings, **Studio**, under **Client forms**: how long links work, and an intro and thank-you text for each form. Leave a text empty to use Zorlo's starter text, shown in grey. The form shows your studio's name and logo, and "Powered by Zorlo" in small print.
