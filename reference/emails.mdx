Every email Zorlo sends, who gets it and what sets it off. Emails never contain prices, fees, client phone numbers, the brief or notes. Every button in an email opens the app behind sign-in. Nothing is confirmed or declined from the email itself.

<Note>
Last checked: 30 October 2026, Zorlo release 2.
</Note>

## Accounts

| Email | Subject | To | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Invitation | "Name invited you to Studio" | The person invited | An owner invites anyone, or an admin invites a shooter. The link works for 7 days. Inviting again sends a fresh link and voids the old one |
| Sign-in code | "Your sign-in code" | The person signing in | After setting a password on an invitation, or when a code is needed. Six digits, expires after 10 minutes, resend after 60 seconds |
| Password reset | Standard reset email | The address entered | Forgot password. A reset signs out every other session |

## Crew offers and reminders

| Email | Subject | To | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offer | "Studio: can you shoot on Date?" | The shooter | An admin puts them on a place. Button: Reply in the app |
| Offer reminder | "Reminder: can you shoot on Date?" | The shooter | Automatically once, when the offer has had no reply for "Remind crew after" hours (checked every hour). Or by hand from Needs attention, Send reminder, at most once an hour |
| Day-before reminder | "Reminder: Event type, Client" | Every confirmed shooter on the event | From 6pm Singapore time the day before, once |

## Changes to an event

| Email | Subject | To | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Date or time changed | "Changed: Event type, Client. Can you still make it?" | Everyone offered, confirmed or reconfirming on the event | The date, start or end time changed. Shows the old and new details. Confirmed crew move to Reconfirm |
| Venue or notes changed | "Updated: Event type, Client on Date" | Confirmed crew | The venue, address, start location or notes for crew changed. Nothing to do |
| Taken off an event | "Update: you're off Event type, Client on Date" | The person removed | An admin took them off an event that is still going ahead, with the admin's note if any |
| Event cancelled | "Cancelled: Event type, Client on Date" | Everyone who held a place | The event or the whole booking was cancelled |

## To the booking's admin

| Email | Subject | To | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Declined or withdrew | "Name declined Event type, Client on Date" or "Name withdrew from ..." | The booking's assigned admin | A shooter declines an offer or withdraws from a confirmed place |
| Morning digest | "N urgent items at Studio today" | The owner (every booking) and each admin (their own bookings). With Editing on, each editor for their own tasks | 8am Singapore time, only on days with something urgent. Lists the Urgent items from Needs attention |

## Client forms

| Email | Subject | To | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Form received | "Name sent their coverage brief" (or booking details, or song list) | The booking's assigned admin, or the owners if there is none | A client sends a form. Says how many changes there are to check. Nothing the client wrote is in the email |

## Editing

Only while Editing is switched on for the studio. See [Editing workspace](/how-to/editing-workspace).

| Email | Subject | To | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Task assigned | "Studio: Highlight film (Couple) is yours" | The person assigned | Staff assign a task to someone. Not sent when an editor assigns themselves |
| Changes requested | "Changes requested on Highlight film (Couple)" | The task's assignee | Staff send a task back from Reviewing with a note. The note is in the email |
| Ready for review | "Ready for review: Highlight film (Couple)" | The booking's assigned admin | The assignee sends a task for review. Includes the review link |
| Completed | "Completed: Highlight film (Couple)" | The assignee, or the booking's admin when the assignee completed it with review off | A task reaches Completed |

These emails carry the task, the couple's names, dates and links only. Never the brief, songs, prices or contact details.

## Rules

- **Staff who shoot are not emailed about their own places.** Owners and admins see them in the app.
- **One email per person per change.** Saving an event emails each affected person once, however many places they hold on it.
- **Studios in read-only mode send nothing.** See [Platform console](/reference/platform-console).
- Emails come from Zorlo's sending domain, not from the studio's address. Replies do not reach the studio: crew use the WhatsApp link on their job page instead.
- Nothing is sent to clients. Client messages and form links are copied and sent by WhatsApp by the studio.
