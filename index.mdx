Zorlo runs a photography or videography studio's bookings, schedule, clients, crew, payments, deliverables and client forms in one place. This guide covers what is live today for the four roles: owners, admins, shooters and editors. Pages were last checked against Zorlo release 2 on 30 October 2026; the editing workspace pages against release 3.
