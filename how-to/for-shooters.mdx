Everything a shooter does in Zorlo, on a phone. Owners and admins who also shoot use the same screens in the Jobs workspace.

<Note>
Last checked: 30 October 2026, Zorlo release 2. Shooters, and staff who shoot.
</Note>

## Find your way around

**My events** has three tabs. **Requests** holds offers waiting for your reply and jobs whose details changed. **Upcoming** holds confirmed jobs, with the next one at the top. **Past** holds finished jobs, one list with the month marked as you scroll. **My details** at the bottom is your profile.

If you work for more than one studio, tap the studio name at the top to switch.

![Upcoming: your next job with its call time, directions, and the coordinator's WhatsApp and phone.](/images/shooter-upcoming.png)

## Reply to an offer

1. Open **Requests**. Each card shows the date, call time, venue and your estimated pay if the studio shows it.
2. Tap **Confirm** to take the job, or **Decline**. Declining asks for an optional reason. A short notice says "Confirmed. It's in Upcoming." or "Declined. The studio has been told."

![A request. Confirm or decline from the card.](/images/shooter-requests.png)

**What happens next.** Confirming blocks that time in your calendar for every studio you work with. The job moves to Upcoming. Declining tells the studio straight away so they can find someone else. If you have not replied after the studio's reminder window (default two days), you get one reminder email.

If you are already busy at that time, Zorlo refuses the confirmation and says so.

![Declining. The reason is optional.](/images/shooter-decline.png)

## Reconfirm after a change

When the studio moves a job's date or time, it comes back under Requests marked "Details changed, please reconfirm", and you get an email with the old and new details. Under the heading, **Was ...** shows the old date, times or venue struck through, so you can see exactly what changed. Your calendar is freed until you confirm again. Tap **Confirm** if you can still make it, or **Decline**.

## Read the job page

Tap **View job** on the next-job card, or any job in the list.

![A job page: call time, venue, pay estimate, notes for crew, the brief, group shots, vendors and people.](/images/shooter-job.png)

From top to bottom:

- **Call time** is when the studio wants you there, before the event starts. Tap **Directions** for maps.
- **About \$X at \$Y an hour** is your estimate, if the studio shows it. Tap the info icon to see how it is worked out. Final pay is agreed with the studio.
- **Notes for crew** is what the admin wrote for you.
- **Coverage brief**, **Group shots** and **Vendors** expand to show the couple's must-capture moments, important people, run sheet, family combinations and the other suppliers on the day. The page remembers which you left open.
- **People** lists the coordinator (tap **Ask on WhatsApp**, or the phone button to call), the booking's contact persons with tap-to-call numbers, and the other crew with their roles.

You never see prices, payments or other crew's rates.

## Withdraw from a job

Only if you must. Scroll to the bottom of the job page and tap **Withdraw from this event**, then give a reason. The studio's admin is emailed at once and the place reopens. A withdrawal less than 48 hours before the event is recorded as late.

## Set the dates you cannot work

1. Open **My details**.
2. Under **Dates I can't work**, enter the **First day** and **Last day** and tap **Add**. For a single day, enter the same date twice.

![My details: contact details and the dates you cannot work.](/images/shooter-me.png)

Every studio you work with sees those days as busy, with no reason given. Studios will not offer you events on them. Tap the cross next to a range to remove it. Owners and admins see your unavailable days on your profile and on the schedule, but cannot change them.

## Update your details

Under **Contact** on My details, change your name or phone and tap **Save**. Your email is set by the studio's invitation and cannot be changed here. Keep your phone number current: it is how the studio reaches you on the day.

## Change the theme or sign out

At the bottom of My details, **Theme** switches between dark, light and your device's setting. **Sign out** ends your session on this device.

## Staff who shoot

If you are an owner or admin with a crew role, you have two workspaces: **Studio** (bookings, schedule and crew) and **Jobs** (your own shoots), plus **Editing** when the editing board is switched on for the studio. A phone opens in Jobs the first time. To switch, tap the studio name at the top of the sidebar, or the top of the **More** tab on a phone, and choose **Switch workspace**. The device remembers your choice.
