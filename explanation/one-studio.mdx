---
title: "Why owners and admins belong to one studio"
description: "A shooter can work for several studios on Zorlo. An owner or admin can belong to exactly one."
---

A shooter can work for several studios on Zorlo. An owner or admin can belong to exactly one. This is deliberate, and the database enforces it, not just the invitation screen.

<Note>
Last checked: 30 October 2026, Zorlo release 2.
</Note>

## The rule

- A person has one account, and one role in each studio they belong to.
- Only shooters may be members of more than one studio.
- An owner or admin belongs to one studio and cannot be a member of any other studio in any role, not even as a shooter.
- Inviting someone to be an owner or admin when they already belong to another studio is refused. Inviting another studio's owner or admin to be a shooter is refused too. The message says which rule applied.

## Why

Owners and admins see everything in their studio: every client, every price, every payment, every crew rate. Zorlo is built for several studios to use the same system, including studios that compete with each other for the same freelance shooters. If one person could be an admin in two studios, or an admin in one and a shooter in another, they would carry one studio's client list, pricing and crew rates into the other, and Zorlo could not stop them. The simplest guarantee is that the people who see everything see it in one place only.

Shooters are different. What they see is limited to their own jobs, and nothing they see in one studio helps or harms another. The only thing that crosses between studios is their busy time, with no detail, so that two studios do not book the same person for the same hour. That is the whole point of letting them belong to several.

## What it means in practice

- When you invite a freelance shooter who already uses Zorlo with another studio, the invitation links to their existing account. They accept with the sign-in they already have and see your studio next to the other in the switcher.
- When you invite an admin, use an email address that is not already an owner or admin elsewhere. If the person runs two studios, they need two accounts with two addresses.
- If an admin leaves to join another studio, remove them from yours first. The other studio can then invite them.
- An owner or admin who also shoots for your own studio is fine: that is one studio. Give them a crew role on their profile and they get the job view.
