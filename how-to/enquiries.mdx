Every enquiry is a booking with the status Enquiry, even one that never converts. That way nothing is lost and every lead has a follow-up date.

<Note>
Last checked: 30 October 2026, Zorlo release 2. Owners and admins.
</Note>

![The Enquiries list: open enquiries and quotes, sorted by follow-up date.](/images/enquiries-list.png)

## Log an enquiry

1. Click **New booking** (from Enquiries, Bookings or Needs attention).
2. Under **Client**, pick Couple, Individual or Company and enter the name and a phone number or email. If the phone or email matches an existing client, Zorlo shows the match: choose it rather than creating a duplicate.
3. Record **How they found you** if you know.
4. Under **Package and price**, choose a package if they asked about one, or leave No package. The price can stay empty.
5. Under **Booking**, leave the status as **Enquiry**, pick the assigned admin and set **Follow up on** to the day you want to chase them.
6. Under **Events**, add what you know: event type, date, times and venue. Leave blank what you do not know.
7. Write a **First note**, for example what they asked and what you sent.
8. Click **Create booking**.

**What happens next.** The enquiry appears in Enquiries and on the schedule as an outline, so you can see it against other work. If you set no follow-up date, Needs attention flags it after the studio's follow-up window (default 3 days) with no activity.

## Add a note

1. Open the enquiry.
2. In **Notes**, type what happened and click **Add note**.

Notes are dated and carry the author's name. They stay with the booking for its whole life. Only the author can edit or delete their note. Adding a note counts as activity and resets the "no activity" clock.

![An enquiry. Status, follow-up date and package sit in the right-hand panel; notes at the bottom.](/images/enquiry-detail.png)

## Set or change the follow-up date

1. Open the enquiry.
2. In the right-hand panel, click **Follow up** and pick a date. Click the cross to clear it.

On that date the enquiry shows in Needs attention as "Follow up" with an **Open** button. A date still ahead keeps it quiet until then.

## Send a quote

1. Open the enquiry and set the **Agreed price** and **Deposit** in the right-hand panel (the deposit is suggested from the studio's percentage).
2. Change **Status** to **Quoted**.

Three days after quoting with no deposit logged, Needs attention shows "Deposit not received" as Soon. Within 7 days of an event it becomes Urgent.

## Confirm the booking

Log the deposit: see [Payments](/how-to/payments). The booking moves to Confirmed and leaves the Enquiries list. Only the owner can set Confirmed by hand without a deposit.

## Mark an enquiry as lost

1. Open the enquiry and click **Mark lost** at the bottom of the right-hand panel. (The Enquiries list also has a Mark lost button on each row.)
2. Choose a **Reason** from the studio's list, for example Price or Booked elsewhere.
3. Add a note if useful and click **Mark lost**.

![Marking an enquiry lost needs a reason.](/images/enquiry-mark-lost.png)

The enquiry moves to **Enquiries, Lost**. Nothing is deleted. The reasons list is edited in Settings, Lost reasons.

## Reopen a lost enquiry

1. Go to **Enquiries** and click **Lost**.
2. Open the enquiry and click **Reopen**.

It goes back to Enquiry with its notes and events intact.

## Find an enquiry

The Enquiries list searches by name, phone and email. Filter by status (Enquiry or Quoted), assigned admin or event date. Sort by follow-up date, client name, price or last activity.

## Reply with a template

If your studio has message templates, open the enquiry and click **Copy message** at the top. Pick **Enquiry reply**, check the filled text, click **Copy** and paste it into WhatsApp. The same button fills the **Booking details request** once the client form link is made. See [Messages](/how-to/messages).
