<Note>
For shooters, on a phone. About 10 minutes. You need the invitation email from your studio.
</Note>

You will get into Zorlo, confirm your first job, read its page and tell the studio the dates you cannot work. After this, the app mostly runs itself: you get an email when something needs you.

## 1. Accept the invitation

1. Open the email "Name invited you to Studio" on your phone and tap the link.
2. Set a password (10 characters or more), then type the 6-digit code that arrives by email. Or tap **Continue with Google** with the same email address.
3. You land on **My events**.

Add Zorlo to your home screen so it opens like an app: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen; in Chrome tap the menu, then Add to Home screen.

## 2. Look around

Three tabs: **Requests** (offers waiting for you), **Upcoming** (confirmed jobs) and **Past**. At the bottom, **My events** and **My details**.

![Upcoming, with the next job at the top.](/images/shooter-upcoming.png)

## 3. Confirm your first job

When the studio offers you a job you get an email, and the job appears under **Requests** with the date, call time, venue and your estimated pay if the studio shows it.

1. Open **Requests**.
2. Read the card. Tap **Confirm**. A notice says "Confirmed. It's in Upcoming."

![A request.](/images/shooter-requests.png)

The job moves to **Upcoming**. That time is now blocked in your calendar for every studio you work with on Zorlo. If you could not do it, you would tap **Decline** instead and the studio would be told at once.

## 4. Read the job page

1. On **Upcoming**, tap **View job** on the next-job card.
2. Scroll through: the **call time** (when to be there), the venue with **Directions**, your pay estimate, the admin's **Notes for crew**, then the couple's brief, group shots and vendors if the studio uses them, and **People**: the coordinator with **Ask on WhatsApp** and a button to call them, the contact persons for the day with tap-to-call numbers, and the other crew.

![A job page.](/images/shooter-job.png)

Everything you need on the day is here. There are no prices or payments, by design.

## 5. Tell the studio when you cannot work

1. Tap **My details**.
2. Under **Dates I can't work**, enter the first and last day of a holiday and tap **Add**.

![My details.](/images/shooter-me.png)

Every studio you work with now sees those days as busy. They will not offer you events then. Nobody sees why.

## 6. Check your phone number

Still on **My details**, make sure your **Phone** is right and tap **Save**. It is how the studio reaches you on the day.

## Done

From now on, Zorlo emails you when you are offered a job, when a job's details change, the day before each job, and once if you forget to reply. Everything you can do is in [For shooters](/how-to/for-shooters).
