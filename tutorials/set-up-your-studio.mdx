<Note>
For the owner. About 30 minutes. You need your invitation email and a phone with an authenticator app.
</Note>

By the end you will have a studio that is ready for its first booking: your account secured, the studio's defaults set, its lists filled in, one package built and your first admin and shooters invited. Follow the steps in order. Each one builds on the last.

## 1. Accept your invitation and secure your account

1. Open the invitation email from Zorlo and click the link.
2. Set a password of at least 10 characters, then enter the 6-digit code Zorlo emails you. Or choose **Continue with Google** with the same address the invitation went to.
3. On **Set up two-step sign-in**, scan the QR code with your authenticator app and enter the code it shows.

You land on **Needs attention**. It is empty, because there is nothing to do yet. That is normal. A **Finish setup** card at the bottom of the sidebar lists four steps (invite crew, add packages, log the first enquiry, offer the first job), ticks them off as you go and disappears when they are done.

![Needs attention once a studio is busy. Yours starts empty.](/images/attention-owner.png)

## 2. Name the studio and set its defaults

1. Click **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Studio**.
2. Check the **Studio name**. Upload a **Logo** if you have one.
3. Set the **Deposit** percentage you normally ask for. The default is 30%.
4. Leave the rest at their defaults for now. You can come back once you have seen how the reminders behave.
5. Click **Save changes**.

![Studio settings.](/images/settings-studio.png)

## 3. Fill in the lists

Zorlo never hard-codes what a studio shoots. A new studio starts with common lists. Check each one and change what you need.

1. **Event types**: Nikah, Reception, Henna, ROM and Pre-wedding to begin with. Add the rest of what you shoot, for example Corporate event.
2. **Crew roles and levels**: check that the roles are Photographer and Videographer (add others if you have them) and the levels are Lead, Second and Third in that order.
3. **Lost reasons**: Price, Date unavailable, No reply and Booked elsewhere to begin with.
4. **Vendor types**: the default list covers make-up artist, decor, catering and the rest. Add any you need.
5. **Deliverable types**: what you hand over after a shoot, for example Photo gallery, Album, Highlight film. For each, say what it waits on (nothing, the song list or the photo selection) and its usual turnaround in days.

![Crew roles and levels.](/images/settings-crew.png)

## 4. Build your first package

1. Settings, **Packages**, then **New package**.
2. Name it after your most common sale, for example "Malay wedding, full coverage". Add a description.
3. Under **Pricing**, choose **Fixed** and add two prices: Weekday and Weekend, with your amounts.
4. Under **Included events**, add the Nikah with its hours and the crew needed: 1 Photographer Lead, 1 Photographer Any level, 1 Videographer Lead. Add the Reception the same way.
5. Under **Deliverables**, add what the package includes, for example 1 Photo gallery and 1 Highlight film.
6. Under **Add-ons**, add one extra you often sell, for example an extra highlight film.
7. Click **Save package**.

![A finished package.](/images/settings-package-fixed.png)

## 5. Invite your admin

1. Go to **Crew** and click **Invite**.
2. Enter their name and email and choose **Admin**.
3. Send. They get an email with a link that works for 7 days. Until they accept, their row shows Invited.

An admin can do everything around bookings and crew. If you want them to edit packages or settings too, switch those on under Settings, **Admin permissions**. Leave them off for now.

## 6. Invite your shooters

1. **Crew**, **Invite**. Enter the name and email and choose **Crew**.
2. Add their crew role, level and hourly rate now, for example Photographer, Lead, 60 an hour. You can add more roles later on their profile.
3. Send. Repeat for each shooter.

Shooters do not need two-step sign-in and can accept from their phone. If a shooter already uses Zorlo with another studio, the invitation links to the account they have.

![A shooter's profile after they accept.](/images/crew-profile.png)

## 7. Decide what a booking holds

Settings, **Booking sections**. Every section is on: Song list, Group shots, Vendors, Coverage brief, Files, Client meetings and Deliverables. Switch off any your studio does not use, for example Client meetings if you never record them. You can switch them back on later and nothing is lost.

## Done

Your studio is ready. Next: [Your first booking, from enquiry to confirmed](/tutorials/first-booking). When you want to know what each setting does, see [Studio settings](/reference/studio-settings) in the Reference section.
