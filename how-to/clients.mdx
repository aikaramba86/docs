The people you work for: couples, individuals and companies. Each client has one page with their details and every booking they have had.

<Note>
Last checked: 30 October 2026, Zorlo release 2. Owners and admins.
</Note>

![The Clients list. Filter by type, sort, or search by name, phone or email.](/images/clients-list.png)

## Add a client

Most clients are created with their first booking (see [Enquiries](/how-to/enquiries)). To add one on their own:

1. Go to **Clients** and click **Add client**.
2. Choose the type: **Couple**, **Individual** or **Company**.
3. Enter the name (and the partner's name for a couple, or the company name and contact name for a company).
4. Enter a phone number or an email. One of the two is required.
5. Optionally record how they found you.
6. Save.

## Avoid a duplicate

When you create a booking and type a phone number or email that matches an existing client, Zorlo shows the match and offers to use that client instead. Pick the existing client unless you are sure it is someone else.

## Edit a client's details

1. Open the client from the list.
2. The right-hand panel holds their type, names, phone, email, addresses, Instagram handles and source. Click a value to change it. Changes save as you go.
3. **Preferences** and **Notes** on the left are free text. Preferences are things that apply to every booking, for example "prefers candid shots". Notes about one booking go on that booking instead.

![A client's page: bookings on the left, details on the right.](/images/client-detail.png)

## Record a key date

Anniversaries, birthdays, the wedding date.

1. Open the client.
2. Next to **Key dates**, click **Add a date**.
3. Give it a label and a date.

## Add an address

1. Open the client.
2. Next to **Addresses**, click **Add an address**.
3. Enter it. A Singapore postal code can be looked up to fill the street.

## See a client's booking history

The **Bookings** list on the client's page shows every booking, including lost and cancelled ones, with its events, package, price and status. Click one to open it.

## Delete a client

Only the owner can delete a client, and only one with no bookings. Open the client and use **Delete** at the bottom of the panel. Clients with bookings are kept so the history stays complete.

## What clients never see

Zorlo has no client login. The only thing a client ever opens is a form you send them by link: their booking details, the coverage brief or the song list, each showing only that booking's details for that form. See [Client forms](/how-to/client-forms). Everything else the studio copies into WhatsApp.
